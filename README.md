## About Me

I'm a software engineer with 7+ years of professional experience building, developing, and maintaining web applications, CMS platforms, eCommerce solutions, and business-focused software.

My work combines traditional software engineering with modern AI technologies. I develop custom WordPress solutions, Shopify storefronts, eCommerce platforms, web applications, AI agents, conversational systems, and automated business workflows.

Over the years, I have worked across the development lifecycle, from understanding business requirements and designing technical solutions to implementation, integrations, deployment, optimization, and ongoing maintenance.

My focus is on building systems that are reliable, maintainable, secure, and useful in real business environments.

I also work with AI-assisted development tools and modern engineering workflows to improve development efficiency without compromising code quality or maintainability.
